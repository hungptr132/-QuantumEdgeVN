01

What the business is

FLC Faros Construction JSC (ROS) was a construction and real-estate contracting arm of the FLC Group, registered in 2011 with VND 1.5 billion in charter capital and listed on HOSE in September 2016. By this snapshot date it traded as one of seven tickers Vietnamese retail forums and financial media grouped together as "cổ phiếu họ FLC" — the "FLC family stocks": FLC, ROS, HAI, AMD, GAB, ART and KLF — under common leadership, with ART (BOS Securities) acting as the group's own affiliated brokerage.

What would have to be true

  • A firm that grew charter capital ~2,867× in five years, then again within 18 months of listing, has a real balance sheet behind that capital rather than one built mostly on paper.
  • A company whose audited profit was erased by 99.96% in the same year it was earned can plausibly guide +115% pre-tax growth the next year with no new disclosed business driving it.
  • Seven correlated "họ FLC" tickers — one already showing a near-identical thin-volume rally pattern (GAB) — reflect independent fundamentals rather than common control of trading.
  • The group's captive brokerage (ART/BOS) financing accounts that trade the other six ecosystem tickers creates no conflict of interest.

A tip, run through the checker

A plausible Zalo-group tip from late December 2021, verified in code against §02–§04 rather than judged.

QUANTUMEDGE · TIP CHECKROS

"ROS sắp bứt phá! Vừa vượt mệnh giá sau 2 năm, kế hoạch lợi nhuận 2022 tăng khủng, cả họ FLC đang kéo trần. Vào hàng sớm trước khi tăng tiếp!" "ROS is about to break out! Just crossed par value after 2 years, the 2022 profit plan is surging, the whole FLC family is hitting ceiling. Get in early before it runs further!"

Claims

ROS crossed above par value; a large forward profit increase is planned; the wider FLC-linked group is rallying together; the rally should continue.

What the data shows

The par-value crossing and group-wide rally are confirmed (§03, §04). The profit-plan claim matches the company's own guidance — no corresponding disclosure found that independently substantiates its operating basis. No disclosure found explaining why seven related tickers move together beyond common ownership — itself a related party flag, not a fundamentals signal.

What it doesn't tell you

Nearly two years below par value before this rally; a ~2,867× capital increase at listing with no sector-median context available; GAB already showing an identical thin-volume pattern a year earlier. No conclusion of "scam" or "don't buy" — only that the excitement isn't yet supported by anything beyond what's flagged above.
