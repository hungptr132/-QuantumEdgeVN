1. HOW WE GOT HERE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The conversation moved through several business ideas before converging:

  - Generic teen online business plan (financial education, not products)
  - Vietnam-focused finance content in two languages (VN + EN)
  - Dropshipping in 2026 — assessed, judged weak edge, time-box only
  - General Vietnam-executable online business ideas
  - Fintech: data product built on the existing HOSE screener
  - Final: a lookup / verification platform for Vietnamese small & mid-caps

Key observation raised repeatedly: every framing landed in the same place —
Vietnamese market data, explained well. That convergence is the edge. The
stated risk is idea-switching, not idea shortage.

Timing note: FTSE Russell upgrade of Vietnam frontier -> secondary emerging
market, effective 21 September 2026. Constituent list published 21 August 2026.
Estimated USD 5–6bn passive inflows over the following 12–18 months.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PRODUCT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ONE LINE
  "Tra cuu bat ky co phieu nao — ke ca nhung ma khong ai phan tich."
  (Look up any stock — including the ones nobody analyses.)

THE PROBLEM
  Vietnamese retail investors receive stock tips via Zalo and Facebook groups
  and have no fast way to check them. Broker research covers VN30; the stocks
  that actually get tipped are small/mid-caps with no coverage in any language.
  The gap is not literacy in the abstract — it is VERIFICATION AT THE MOMENT
  OF DECISION.

THE USP
  Coverage where coverage doesn't exist. Breadth on small-caps is a content
  moat: at 200 tickers, a competitor needs 200 too. Blue-chip analysis is
  commodity; this isn't. Small-cap-specific risks (thin liquidity, dilution
  history, related-party dealings) are the differentiating signals.

AUDIENCE
  Primary   — Vietnamese retail investors
  Secondary — English-language readers riding the FTSE reclassification


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PRODUCT LAYERS (build order)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 0 — Schema (invisible, decides everything)
  Objects: Ticker, Company, Sector, Flag, Filing, Metric, User, Watchlist.
  Every surface is a query over these. Get it right once = new features in a
  day. Get it wrong = rebuild.

LAYER 1 — Ticker page (the atom)
  - What the business does, 2–3 sentences, plain Vietnamese
  - 5 metrics + sector median + the stock's own 3-year range
  - Active red flags, each linked to the filing or data row as evidence
  - "What would have to be true for this to work"
  - Explicit "khong cong bo" where data is missing — this is part of the value
  - Review stamp: date + reviewer + personal position disclosure
  Track: most-viewed tickers -> that's the upgrade-to-handwritten list.

LAYER 2 — Sector pages + flag-list pages
  Auto-generated from the same schema. Sector pages age better and rank
  better than obscure tickers. Flag lists ("all HOSE companies with interest
  coverage under 2x") are inherently shareable.

LAYER 3 — Search
  The real front door. Must handle: uppercase/lowercase tickers, company names
  with and without diacritics, forum nicknames, single-character typos. The
  alias table built here is reused by the bot and the tip parser.

LAYER 4 — Accounts + watchlist
  The moment it becomes a platform rather than a site. Technically light,
  strategically heavy: tells you who cares about which tickers, which directs
  all future effort allocation.

LAYER 5 — Alerts
  The ONLY retention mechanic in the whole list. Fires on: flag state change,
  important new disclosure (dilution, auditor change, insider sale), metric
  crossing a threshold. Key insight: the STATE-CHANGE EVENT is more
  interesting than the static snapshot.

LAYER 6 — Zalo/Telegram bot
  Thin client over the same API. Lives in the app where tips actually arrive —
  no context switch required.

LAYER 7 — Tip parser
  Architecturally an INPUT layer, not a page: free text in, a view of
  Layer 1 out.

CROSS-CUTTING
  - Glossary: every metric on every page links to a definition. Cheap, and
    this is the real financial-literacy component.
  - Share cards: every page exports an image. Distribution channel is message
    forwarding, so the product must be forwardable.

REVISED ORDER (if platform, not publication)
  Layer 1 (10 tickers) -> Layer 6 (bot) -> Layers 4–5 -> then 2–3 once
  traffic justifies SEO work. Ticker pages are read-once-and-leave; without
  watchlist and alerts you have a dictionary, not a platform.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AGENT SYSTEM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
  Agents may READ, EXTRACT, CROSS-CHECK, and EXPRESS.
  Agents may NOT JUDGE.

AGENT 1 — Event extraction
  In:  HOSE disclosures, IR pages, RSS media, vnstock company news
  Out: {event_type, ticker, date, severity, source_url, excerpt}
  Closed enum for event_type: dilution, auditor_change, insider_sale,
  related_party, profit_warning, board_change, share_pledge, none.
  Closed enum is critical — free-text categories break everything downstream.
  Runs daily, writes to the events table. No commentary, no price-impact
  guessing, no sentiment scores.
  Role: fuels the alerting layer. For small-caps this event set is where the
  real risk lives.

AGENT 2 — Analysis expression
  Division of labour is the point:
    Python (deterministic) — thresholds, formulas, flag rules, sector medians
                             -> fact sheet JSON
    LLM (expression only)  — turns the fact sheet into plain Vietnamese
  LLM may use ONLY numbers present in the fact sheet; every figure traceable
  to a field; missing data written as "khong cong bo". Machine validation
  rejects drafts containing unsourced numbers.
  Reason: reproducibility is what's being sold. Same ticker + same data must
  produce the same conclusion every time.

AGENT 3 — Tip parser (the standout feature)
  1. Extract -> {ticker, claims[], price_target, timeframe, tone}
     Claim types: valuation, catalyst/news, technical, insider, vague hype
  2. Verify IN CODE — catalyst claims against Agent 1's event table;
     price targets -> implied P/E and EV/EBITDA vs sector median and the
     stock's own 3-year range
  3. Fire ALL flags for that ticker, including ones the tip never mentioned —
     often the most valuable output
  4. Three blocks out: what the tip claims / what the data shows /
     what it doesn't tell you
  5. Render as a shareable card
  Rules: no match = "no corresponding disclosure found", never "false".
  Never conclude "scam" or "don't buy".
  Log every parsed tip — which tickers get pumped, with what language, when.
  Nobody else will have that dataset.

AGENT 4 — Portfolio inspection (NOT construction)
  User pastes their existing holdings. System DESCRIBES:
    - sector concentration
    - total active flags across the portfolio
    - overlapping risk (e.g. multiple names on one commodity cycle)
    - share of thin-liquidity small-caps
  No buy/sell suggestions, no target weights. Describing what exists is a
  tool; prescribing what should exist is SSC-licensed advice.

AGENT 5 — Liquidity risk (narrowed TA)
  Pure code, no LLM: 20-session average volume, zero-trade sessions over
  3 months, price range, bid-ask spread, estimated sessions to exit a given
  position size. Framed as RISK, not signal. No MACD/RSI, no buy signals.
  For small-caps, thin liquidity is the biggest invisible retail risk — so
  this fits the USP.

FLOW
  Sources
    -> Agent 1 (event extraction)
    -> Events table + financials + price/liquidity  [Postgres]
    -> Deterministic layer: metrics -> flags -> fact sheet JSON
        |-> Agent 2 -> ticker page -> machine validation -> human review -> publish
        |-> Agent 3 -> tip analysis card
        |-> Agent 4 -> portfolio report
        |-> Agent 5 -> liquidity block (embedded in ticker page)
  Loop closes: share card -> forwarded into Zalo group -> next tip pasted in.
  That loop is the distribution, and the only self-growing part of the system.

BUILD ORDER
  Agent 5 (pure code, one evening) -> Agent 2 -> Agent 3 -> Agent 1 -> Agent 4
  Honest advice: don't build all five. Build Agent 3 in a weekend with ONE
  checker, post it to the community, see if it gets forwarded.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE 7-STEP PORTFOLIO FLOW — ASSESSED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Proposed: profile -> screen 10 -> narrow to 5 by news/sentiment -> TA entry
price -> position sizing -> 3 return projections -> save & track.

VERDICT ON EACH

  Step 1  Preference intake      NO MODEL (optional small LLM for free-text
                                 intake; user confirms parsed form)
  Step 2  Screener               CODE — existing Python screen over cached
                                 VN100
  Step 3  News/filings           THE ONE REAL LLM — event extraction, NOT
                                 sentiment. Vietnamese financial media is thin
                                 and often promotional; sentiment scores there
                                 are noise dressed as signal. Code (not the
                                 model) does the 10 -> 5 cut using written
                                 rules.
  Step 4  Entry price            CODE — support/resistance, MAs, ATR bands are
                                 arithmetic. LLMs are unreliable at chart math
                                 with zero capability gain. Output a RANGE with
                                 assumptions shown, not a single number.
  Step 5  Position sizing        CODE — equal-weight / inverse-vol / capped
                                 concentration + Step 1 constraints, rounded to
                                 lot sizes.
  Step 6  Scenarios              CODE — derive from historical multiples
                                 ("at 3-year low P/E this implies X"), never
                                 modelled returns, never probabilities, no time
                                 horizon attached.
  Step 7  Tracking               CODE + optional LLM monthly plain-Vietnamese
                                 summary driven strictly from snapshot diffs.

  HONEST COUNT: one agent. Step 3 is the only place a language model does work
  code can't.

TWO STRUCTURAL WARNINGS
  - VN100 is large/mid-cap — using it quietly abandons the small-cap USP.
    Not wrong, but it's a different product. VN100 rebalances semi-annually,
    not monthly.
  - The word "client" is the problem, not the tech. Steps 4–6 output an entry
    price, a share count, and a projection for a named person = personalised
    investment advice = SSC-licensed territory. Two workable versions:
      (a) PERSONAL TOOL — built for yourself, own money, no regulatory issue
      (b) SELF-DIRECTED TOOL — user sets criteria and sizes their own
          positions; you provide the calculator, they make every decision
    What doesn't work is a system that decides for someone else.

6. DATA & INFRASTRUCTURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SOURCES
  vnstock     — financials, prices, company news (wrapper over TCBS/SSI
                endpoints; unaffiliated, can break without warning — cache
                everything)
  RSS         — VnExpress, CafeF, VietStock, Tuoi Tre, Thanh Nien via
                feedparser
  HOSE + IR   — official disclosures; hardest to parse, highest value, and the
                actual differentiator for small-caps
  Scraping    — last resort only; respect robots.txt, rate-limit, cache

COPYRIGHT LINE
  Store headline + link + publisher + timestamp ONLY. Never article bodies.
  Display headline + link + your own one-line note. News is an INPUT TO FLAGS,
  not a page section — that keeps the product in "verification tool" rather
  than the crowded "news aggregator" category.

PIPELINE
  Ingest (raw, timestamped, never re-fetched)
    -> deterministic extraction (never let a model retype a number off a PDF)
    -> structured fact sheet per ticker
    -> LLM draft from that fact sheet only
    -> machine validation (rejects any number not in the fact sheet, ticker
       mismatches, missing sections, wrong length)
    -> human review queue (approve / edit / reject; rejections logged, and
       that log is how the prompt improves)
    -> static publish with reviewed_by + reviewed_at stamp
  Alerts on: new filing for a covered ticker, flag state change, page stale
  past 90 days. Re-run only what changed.

STACK
  Next.js + Postgres + Vercel, static generation (traffic costs nothing,
  nothing breaks during exams). Python pipeline writes to the same database,
  fully separate from the web app. Git from day one, prompts versioned.

TWO THINGS THAT WILL BITE
  - Extraction from Vietnamese financial PDFs is the hardest engineering here
    and always takes ~3x longer than expected.
  - Every stage must be idempotent and re-runnable — you WILL change a rule at
    ticker 80 and need to regenerate all 80.

SECURITY NOTE
  An API key was pasted in chat during this conversation. Rotate it. Keep
  credentials in a gitignored .env file — never in messages, never in a repo.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. STRATEGY & GUARDRAILS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

COVERAGE — THE BARBELL
  10 hand-written flagship pages proving capability, plus automated shallow
  coverage clearly marked as such. Promote shallow -> deep based on what people
  ask for. Demand tells you where to spend effort.
  Automate the DRAFTING, never the VERIFYING. The "reviewed by [name] on
  [date]" stamp is the product. Fully automated output at scale is exactly
  what a competitor with more compute replicates next month — and one
  hallucinated fact about a small-cap, in public, ends the trust being sold.

REGULATORY GUARDRAILS
  - Education and context, never recommendations (SSC-licensed territory)
  - Disclose personal positions on any covered ticker
  - Unmatched claim = "no disclosure found", never "false"
  - Negative catalogue: strictly factual and sourced (defamation risk)
  - No guaranteed returns, never handle other people's money

CADENCE
  Weekly cycle: Monday pick ONE thing -> midweek build -> Friday publish
  publicly -> weekend log what people asked that you couldn't answer.
  Small enough to survive a bad week at university. The public deadline is
  what keeps it moving.

DECISION LOG
  One file, one line per decision: what was chosen, why, what would reverse it.
  Includes kill criteria. Also the artifact that makes the project legible to
  admissions and employers.
